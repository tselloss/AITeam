# Pre-sales & customer pipeline

`ceo` routes prospect technical questions to `sales-engineer` (scoping memo → `docs/sales/`,
handing off to `cpo` for roadmap gaps or `dev-lead` once it's a committed task) and
existing-customer account questions to `customer-success` (account summary →
`docs/customers/`, handing off to `product-owner` for feature requests or `support-engineer`
for bug reports). `cpo` and `cfo` route usage/cost-data questions to `data-analyst`
(findings memo → `docs/intelligence/`). None of the three bypass the existing pipeline —
they feed into it at its normal entry points rather than shipping or deciding anything
themselves.
